Privacy Policy

Nirnoy Reel Studio · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Nirnoy Reel Studio ("the Studio", "we") is an internal tool operated by Nirnoy (nirnoy.app), a Bangladesh-based exam-preparation service. The Studio is used only by authorised members of the Nirnoy team to create short multiple-choice-question videos and publish them to social media accounts that Nirnoy owns or manages. It is not offered to the general public.

What information we access

When a team member connects a social media account (for example a Facebook Page), the platform asks them to grant permissions. With those permissions we access only:

  • The list of Pages / accounts the person manages (ID, name and profile picture), so they can choose which ones to connect.
  • An access token for each account they choose, so the Studio can publish videos to it.
  • The status and public link of the videos we publish, so the Studio can show whether a post succeeded.

We do not read private messages, followers' personal data, friend lists, or any content other than what is listed above. We also store the email address of the signed-in team member to record who created or published a reel.

How we use it

  • To show the connected accounts in the Studio.
  • To upload and publish the videos and captions that a team member explicitly selects, at the time they choose.
  • To show the result (published, scheduled or failed) and the link to each post.

We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling, analytics resale or training machine-learning models, and we never sell or rent it.

Storage and security

Data is stored on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database and R2 storage). Access tokens are encrypted with AES-GCM before they are saved, and the encryption key is kept as a separate secret. The Studio itself is behind Cloudflare Access, so only approved team members can sign in.

Sharing

We share data only with the platform it came from (for example, sending a video to Facebook to publish it) and with Cloudflare as our hosting provider. We disclose information to authorities only when required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Removing a connected account in the Studio deletes its access token immediately and cancels its scheduled posts.
  • You can also revoke the Studio's access at any time from the platform itself (on Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites).
  • Posts that were already published stay on the platform until they are deleted there.
  • See our data deletion instructions, or email us to delete everything we hold about your account.

Children

The Studio is used only by adult members of the Nirnoy team and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
