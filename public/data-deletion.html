Data Deletion Instructions

Nirnoy Reel Studio · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Nirnoy Reel Studio stores only the ID, name, profile picture and encrypted access token of the social media accounts you connect, plus the status and links of posts it published. You can have this data deleted in any of these ways:

1. Remove the account in the Studio

Open the Studio's Channels page and press Remove next to the account. Its access token is deleted immediately and any scheduled posts for it are cancelled.

2. Remove the app on Facebook

On Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find Nirnoy Reel Studio and remove it. The stored token stops working at once. To also delete the stored record, use option 1 or 3.

3. Email us

Send a request to help@nirnoy.app with the name of the Page or account. We will delete all data we hold about it, including publish history, and confirm by email within 7 days.

Posts that were already published remain on the platform until you delete them there.
